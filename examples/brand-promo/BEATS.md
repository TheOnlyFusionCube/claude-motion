# Sprout: beat sheet

A 14 s brand promo for a fictional savings app. Near-black and one green; the green coin carries the film.

| t | subject | action | framing | words on screen | carries into next by | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2.1 | six glossy coins | burst out from depth to a ring round the line, tumbling | wide | Grow (leaf) what you save (coin) | the others fly off; the green coin comes forward | whoosh, a pop per coin |
| 2.1–3.9 | the green coin | becomes the logo: rays turn behind it; the name slams in | coin left, name right | Sprout — Savings that grow on their own. | the coin shrinks to the centre | riser, impact, groove |
| 3.9–6.2 | an orbit | rings draw round the coin; three planets travel with labels | centred | Round-ups · Goals · Rewards | the orbit blows out; the dashboard swings in | whoosh, pops |
| 6.2–8.6 | a tilted dashboard | bars grow, the balance counts up, 4.1% → 5.6% flips up in front | 3D, steep to gentle | $12,480 · 5.6% | the dashboard falls away | swipe, roll, ding |
| 8.6–10.35 | claims | two red ✕ pills land and shake; the green ✓ pill arrives | centred stack | No hidden fees · No lock-ins · Just growth. | the pills fold into the URL field | thuds, chime |
| 10.35–14.2 | URL → logo | the URL types in a glowing field; two half-moons slide in round the name | centred | sprout.money · Sprout — Start growing today. | — (end) | ticks, riser, impact, chord |

Techniques: `three-kit` (`shapes.coin` + `svgTexture` faces, `view3d`, `project`), `kit-unit` icons inside a
sentence, conic-gradient rays, SVG orbits, `kit.space` dashboard, `pill()` claims, `kit.typist`.
