# Sources and data caveats

All numbers, hub positions, labels, times and movements are invented example data written solely for a reproducible, fictional animation. They **must not** be read as real courier logistics or operational recommendations. No real person's or company's product, logo, city map, brand visuals or screenshots are included. The video visibly says "SYNTHETIC DATA / CONCEPT FILM".

The film's low-poly buildings, 3D model, illustration and original synthesized audio are generated by the included `build.py`; no externally sourced third-party stock assets, maps, voice recordings or music are required.
