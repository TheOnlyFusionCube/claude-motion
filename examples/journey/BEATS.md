# Delivered: beat sheet

A 22.5 s concept film about something abstract: how a message crosses the world. It's the reference
for **abstract stories without slides**. One physical subject (the message, then its words) travels
through one world (two phones on a night map), and the camera follows it from finger scale to map scale
and back. There are no headings: the only words on screen are the message, a millisecond counter, the
reply and the end card. Take the technique, not the look or the story.

**Spine:** the message. Typed → sent → split into words → across the ocean (one word takes the long
way) → reassembled on the other phone → answered → "Delivered."
