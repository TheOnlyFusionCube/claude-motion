# Make it move: beat sheet

A 12.5 s kinetic poster reel at 128 bpm. Black, acid lime and chrome; a small HUD as the poster's frame.

| t | subject | action | framing | words on screen | carries into next by | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2.3 | a wall of type | rows scroll in alternate directions, solid and outline; the wall turns and creeps | full bleed, tilted | the rows | the rows whip out; the acid band swells to fill the frame | hats, kick; riser |
| 2.3–4.6 | a chrome knot | it springs in and turns; FORM. slams in letter by letter, WITHOUT LIMITS. slides in | acid poster, type left, knot right | FORM. WITHOUT LIMITS. | type leaves upward; black floods out of the knot | impact + crash, the groove |
| 4.6–6.85 | the knot's surface, as particles | they burst, swirl and settle into a word | black, centred | MOVE (in particles) | the word rains down | impact, sparkle, swell |
| 6.85–9 | a field of blocks | the rain lands; a ripple runs out; a chrome ball rolls through, pressing them | the camera follows the rain down, top-down, then tilts into perspective | — | an acid wipe from below | whoosh, pluck run, thud, roll |
| 9.3–12.5 | a chrome star | it drops in and swings; STAY / LOUD. slam in | acid poster, slow pull back | STAY LOUD. | — (end) | riser, impact, chord |

Techniques: `kit.marquee` (+ whip-out `extraPx`), `TextFx` chars with `eob`, `three-kit` (`view3d`, `mat.chrome`,
`shapes.knot`/`star`, `particles` + `textPoints`, `field`), `kit.iris` from a projected 3D point, motion blur fast spans.
