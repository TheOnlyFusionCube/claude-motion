# Meridian: beat sheet

A 34 s launch film for a **fictional** bike-share operations tool (invented data). It's the reference
for structure and finish in this skill. Borrow the techniques, not the look or the story.

**Spine:** one question, followed all the way through. The title is the question; it lands in the
app; the answer is a chart; the chart's dip is where we push through into the city; the city
shows the dip happening; a comment proposes a fix; the replay shows the fix working; the comment's
pin becomes the logo's dot.

**Look:** warm paper ground, ink, muted grey. Cobalt = bikes, vermilion = empty (two data colours,
each with one job). Instrument Serif display over Inter. The 3D is white clay with soft shadows, so the
data colours carry it.
