# Everything moves: beat sheet

A 13.6 s motion-design reel. No story: a run of craft moves where **each one becomes the next**.
One accent (electric blue) on a warm grey studio, then a cream grid. Real motion blur
(`motionBlur`, with 16–32 samples on the whips).
