# Lumen: beat sheet

A 12.6 s app promo for a fictional app. The app icon carries the film.

| t | subject | action | framing | words on screen | carries into next by | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1.2 | the icon | lands with overshoot over a soft blurred echo of its mark | centred on white | — | the ground cuts to black and the icon flies to its slot | pop, bell |
| 1.2–2.75 | the store page | builds around the icon; Open is tapped and stretches into a field | 2.3× page, cropped | (UI only) | the button IS the field | whoosh, click, swipe |
| 2.75–4.25 | the field | a prompt is typed and sent | centred | "Plan my week around the gym" | the field folds back into the icon | ticks, pop |
| 4.25–5.45 | the home screen | the field becomes the icon (badge 3); a tap; the frame floods sky blue from it | 2.5×, the icon at centre | (labels) | the flood is the next ground | whoosh, click, riser |
| 5.45–8.85 | glossy pills | Write, Plan, Solve: each types itself with a pop and drops back as the next arrives; icon chips drift round | centred | Write · Plan · Solve | the last pill rises away | groove, a tick per letter, chords |
| 8.85–12.6 | icon + wordmark | the icon lands, slides aside, the name types in, the line rises | centred | Lumen — Think out loud. | — (end) | kick, pop, chord, marimba |

Techniques: `ui/devices.js` (`appIcon`, `storeListing`, `chatInput`, `homeScreen`, `pill`, `glyph`), `kit.morph`
(button → field → icon), `kit.typist`, `kit.iris`, `TextFx` chars with `eob`.
