Built for Autonomous Motion Craft

A deterministic engine designed from first principles to defeat the "Deck Trap" and produce authentic, fluid motion picture films.

Deterministic Stage: render(t)

Every element's state is a pure function of time. Jump to any frame, scrub back and forth, or render 60fps in parallel without drift or memory leaks.

CSS 3D & Three.js Viewports

Combine crisp subpixel DOM text under perspective cameras with hardware-accelerated Three.js materials, particles, and parametric geometry.

Multi-Sample Motion Blur

Real temporal sub-frame integration with 180° shutter angle emulation. Whip pans and fast smash transitions smear cinematically without ghosting artifacts.

Offline Audio Synthesizer

Frame-exact programmatic audio engine: 8 melodic instruments, 13 synthesized percussion sounds, and 13 UI foley effects mixed to -16 LUFS standards.

Automated Film Diagnostics

Built-in review scripts measure 2-second visual change velocity (14–30% threshold), track focal travel, and flag frozen air or unreadable text.

6 Curated Style Presets

Instant visual coherence with turnkey style systems: sketch, bubbles, editorial, bold, midnight, and showcase.
